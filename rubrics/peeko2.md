LinkedIn Scoring Rubric — Performance Marketer (User Acquisition)

Company: Peeko · Shortlist score: 36 points maximum · Provisional shortlist cutoff: 24.30 points

## 1. Global Scoring Rules

Score only explicit profile evidence. Attribute work to the candidate only when the profile identifies their action or responsibility.

Score Evidence and Recency independently for LC-2 through LC-5. LC-1 uses the platform-specific scoring rules in Section 6. Use the strongest qualifying source once. Repeated claims and overlapping roles do not add points.

A fact may support multiple criteria when it independently meets each definition. If a higher score requires an assumption, use the lower supported score.

Google Ads and Meta Ads are the primary platform requirements. Other advertising platforms do not earn LC-1 points. Work on other platforms can support LC-2 through LC-5 only when it independently satisfies their definitions, but does not replace Google or Meta evidence. Product marketing, organic marketing and affiliate work alone do not establish Google or Meta paid acquisition capability.

Evidenced Duration is not scored. Do not apply a minimum experience requirement.

Report missing information as Unverified. Warnings do not change points.

## 2. Fields to Use

| Profile JSON | Use |
|---|---|
| work_experience[].description, summary (About), headline | Campaign work, conversion outcomes, funnel improvements and metrics |
| work_experience[].role, role-specific tool lists and work_experience[].skills[].name | Apply the source rules below. Explicit advertising platform names can support LC-1 platform credit. |
| Global Skills and skills attached to Experience | LC-1 platform-name credit for explicit advertising platform names. Relevant paid-metric names can support LC-5 keyword credit. Explicit app campaign references can support the LC-3 mention-only tier. |
| work_experience[].start, work_experience[].end, current status and company | Recency, date quality and deduplication |
| Name and profile URL | Identification only |

Merge duplicate Experience records. Employer names, seniority and education establish no campaign evidence. Global Skills and attached Experience skills can support LC-1 platform-name credit, LC-3 app campaign mention-only credit and LC-5 metric-keyword credit.

## 3. Evidence · 0–4 Points

Use the highest qualifying source score. The source must establish what the criterion requires.

| Strongest qualifying source | Points |
|---|---:|
| Experience description explicitly describes the candidate personally performing the qualifying work | 4 |
| About or Headline explicitly identifies the capability | 3 |
| Experience title explicitly identifies the capability | 2 |
| Skill attached to a specific Experience entry explicitly identifies the capability | 1 |
| No qualifying evidence | 0 |

The source ladder sets the available points for LC-2 through LC-5. LC-1 uses the platform-specific rules in Section 6: an identifiable platform mention earns 4 points per platform, and explicit hands-on paid acquisition work earns 7 points per platform. An advertising platform name alone does not establish hands-on work.

LC-2 may score an explicit claim of paid campaign ownership or management even when the profile does not describe planning, execution and optimisation separately. A title, tool list, skill tag or bare keyword does not establish hands-on work on its own. Award 3 Evidence points for an explicit About or Headline description of qualifying work, such as “Managed paid Google Ads campaigns.” Award zero Recency from that statement alone unless the work is linked to a qualifying dated Experience.

LC-3 can score an explicit mention of app acquisition campaigns under its mention-only tier, even when hands-on work is not established. For its higher tiers, hands-on paid campaign work must be evidenced. LC-2 and LC-3 can score paid campaign work on an unnamed platform when their own tier requirements are met.

For LC-4 and LC-5, award 4 Evidence points for a qualifying Experience description and 3 for qualifying About or Headline evidence. For the LC-5 keyword-only tier, award 1 Evidence point wherever the qualifying keyword appears, including in an Experience description or Skills. A keyword alone earns zero Recency.

## 4. Recency · 0–3 Points

Use the latest dated Experience that establishes the qualifying work.

| Most recent qualifying use | Points |
|---|---:|
| Current or ended 0–12 months ago | 3 |
| Ended 13–36 months ago | 2 |
| Ended 37–60 months ago | 1 |
| Ended more than 60 months ago or qualifying evidence is undated | 0 |

For LC-2 through LC-5, Recency requires a dated Experience entry whose description establishes qualifying work. Experience titles, tool lists, skill tags, About and Headline cannot establish Recency on their own. The latest job date does not carry over to work described elsewhere. LC-1 does not use Recency points.

## 5. Evidenced Duration · Not Scored

Evidenced Duration does not contribute points to LC-1 through LC-5. Do not calculate qualifying months, apply a minimum experience threshold or reduce a score because the candidate has fewer than 24 or 36 months of experience.

Internships and other professional work may provide qualifying evidence when the criterion's requirements are met. Their duration does not add or remove points. Recency and evidence requirements continue to apply where specified.

## 6. Capability Definitions

All five criteria contribute to the 36-point shortlist score.

### LC-1 · Acquisition Campaign Management · /14

Score Google and Meta separately, up to 7 points each. Add the two platform scores. Use the highest applicable level for each platform; do not add levels within a platform.

| Platform evidence | Points per platform |
|---|---:|
| Explicit hands-on paid acquisition campaign work on Google Ads or Meta Ads | 7 |
| Explicit Google Ads or Meta Ads advertising platform mention, without clear hands-on campaign work | 4 |
| No identifiable Google Ads or Meta Ads advertising platform evidence | 0 |

Both platforms mentioned without hands-on evidence: 4 + 4 = 8 points. Hands-on paid acquisition work on one platform and a mention of the other: 7 + 4 = 11 points. Hands-on paid acquisition work on both platforms: 7 + 7 = 14 points.

Hands-on work can be established through an explicit Experience description or a clear statement in About or Headline that attributes the work to the candidate. The platform mentions and hands-on work may come from separate roles. No additional Evidence, Recency or Duration points are applied to LC-1.

Google Ads, AdWords, Google App Campaigns and Universal App Campaigns (UAC) count as Google. Meta Ads, Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads count as Meta. Facebook and Instagram together still count as one platform. Launching, running, managing or optimising named paid acquisition campaigns qualifies. Organic social and generic digital marketing do not qualify.

Amazon Ads, Flipkart Ads, affiliate marketing, product marketing, generic app promotions and other advertising platforms earn zero LC-1 points. A reference to Google or Facebook without an identifiable paid advertising context earns zero. Skills or platform mentions alone earn no LC-2 or LC-4 points. App campaign mentions can earn LC-3 mention-only credit, and metric keywords can earn the LC-5 keyword tier.

### LC-2 · Campaign Ownership, Creation and Optimisation · /7

| Tier | Coefficient | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Campaign ownership | ×1.00 | The candidate explicitly owned paid campaigns or was accountable for their management. A clear statement of ownership qualifies; the profile does not need to describe planning, execution and optimisation separately. |
| Campaign creation | ×0.85 | The candidate personally built or set up a paid campaign. A launch supports this tier when their role in building the campaign is clear. |
| Campaign optimisation | ×0.70 | The candidate personally improved an existing paid campaign through a specific change, such as refining targeting, adjusting bids or restructuring ad sets. |
| Campaign management | ×0.60 | The candidate explicitly ran or managed paid campaigns, but the profile does not establish the higher tiers. |
| No qualifying work | ×0 | A generic marketing title, platform name, reporting, spend monitoring, asset creation or a team result alone earns zero. |

Use the highest supported tier. Multiply the Evidence + Recency score by the tier coefficient. A clear claim of paid campaign ownership qualifies for the ownership tier, with Evidence and Recency scored only from the sources that support it. Quantified results are scored separately under LC-5.

### LC-3 · App and Website Conversion Outcomes · /5

Select the highest supported tier below. For hands-on tiers, multiply the normalised Evidence + Recency score by the criterion maximum and tier coefficient under Section 8. The mention-only tier awards a fixed 1 point.

| Tier | Coefficient | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| App conversions achieved | ×1.00 | Hands-on paid campaign work tied to achieved app installs, in-app sign-ups, in-app purchases or another named app conversion. |
| Website conversions achieved | ×0.70 | Hands-on paid campaign work tied to achieved web sign-ups, form submissions, website purchases or another named website conversion. |
| App campaign work only | ×0.50 | Hands-on paid campaign work with an explicit app objective and no qualifying conversion result stated. |
| Website campaign work only | ×0.30 | Hands-on paid campaign work with a website or unspecified destination and no qualifying conversion result for a known destination. |
| App campaign mention only | Fixed 1.00 point | App acquisition campaigns, Google App Campaigns or an equivalent explicit paid app advertising objective are mentioned, but hands-on work is not established. |
| No qualifying work | 0 | No qualifying paid campaign work or app campaign mention is evidenced. |

A reported conversion rate, a conversion count or a statement that the campaign generated the named conversion can establish achievement when tied to the candidate's work and a known destination. Targets, clicks and traffic alone earn campaign-work credit when hands-on work is described. Quantified results are not required for the campaign-work tiers.

Google App Campaigns (UAC) establish an app objective when hands-on work is described. Meta, Facebook and Instagram ads qualify for the app tiers when the profile explicitly identifies an app objective or conversion. The same website and unspecified-destination rules apply to Google and Meta. For an unspecified destination, use the website campaign-work tier and mark the destination unverified in the notes. Generic app development or product marketing without a paid acquisition context does not qualify for the mention-only tier.

LC-3 uses the conversion outcome to select a tier. LC-5 scores the metric evidence itself. LC-4 evaluates work on a specific funnel step.

### LC-4 · Funnel Optimisation & Conversion Improvement · /3

| Tier | Coefficient | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Measured improvement | ×1.00 | The candidate links a specific change they made or helped implement to a measurable improvement at a named funnel step. Example “Simplified onboarding and raised install-to-signup conversion from 18% to 25%.” |
| Change implemented | ×0.67 | The candidate made or helped implement a specific change to improve a named funnel step. Example “Changed the landing page to address a click-to-signup drop-off.” |
| Funnel analysis | ×0.33 | The candidate analysed a named funnel step or identified a specific drop-off. |
| No qualifying evidence | ×0 | No specific funnel analysis, change or measured result tied to the candidate’s contribution is shown. |

Use the highest supported tier. A conversion rate alone earns zero. Onboarding qualifies when the work targets a specific step such as signup, setup or first purchase. Install volume alone does not establish progress through those steps. A measured improvement earns the highest tier, but an implemented change or analysis does not require quantified results.

### LC-5 · Paid Campaign Metrics · /7

Examples include ROAS, CAC, CPI, CPA, CPL, CTR, CVR, paid installs, qualified leads, paid campaign revenue, campaign spend and acquisition volumes. Require a clear paid-campaign meaning where the metric can also describe unrelated work.

| Tier | Coefficient | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Quantified improvement | ×1.00 | A specific paid-campaign action by the candidate is linked to an achieved, quantified improvement in a named metric. Example “Adjusted bids and increased ROAS from 2× to 3×.” |
| Quantified campaign result | ×0.85 | The candidate links their paid-campaign work to a specific achieved number, including ROAS, CAC, CPI, campaign spend, installs, conversions or acquisition volumes, without needing to show a before-and-after improvement. |
| Hands-on metric work | ×0.60 | The candidate monitored, analysed or used a named paid-campaign metric in their work. A quantified result is optional. Example “Monitored ROAS weekly and adjusted bids.” |
| Keyword only | ×0.30 | A relevant metric is explicitly named, with no clear work tied to it. A target or standalone value without attributable work fits here. Set Evidence to 1 and Recency to 0, for 0.30 points. |
| No qualifying evidence | ×0 | No relevant paid-campaign metric is named. Platform names, generic analytics terms and unlabelled numbers alone earn zero. |

Use the highest supported tier. Score a named funnel-step improvement under LC-4. A distinct paid-campaign metric from the same initiative can earn LC-5 points. General business numbers, unrelated revenue figures and non-paid marketing outcomes do not qualify without a clear paid-campaign connection.

## 7. Date Normalisation

Use stated month and year, and use the evaluation date for Present roles. Use a clear written date when dates are incomplete. Written duration alone cannot establish Recency.

When only years are available, use the end year for Recency by comparing it with the evaluation year. Do not estimate qualifying months or assign Duration points. Use only dated work that meets the relevant evidence requirements.

Ignore contradictory, impossible or future dates. Use the period that can be supported and return a Date-Quality Warning when dates cannot be used reliably.

## 8. Calculation

LC-1: Google platform score (0, 4 or 7) + Meta platform score (0, 4 or 7), following Section 6.

LC-2 through LC-5: ((Evidence + Recency) / 7) × criterion maximum × tier coefficient, following each criterion’s rules. LC-3 mention-only tier is fixed at 1.00 point and does not use this formula. LC-5 keyword-only tier yields 0.30 points.

Final Score (/36): LC-1 + LC-2 + LC-3 + LC-4 + LC-5.

Provisional cutoff: Compare the unrounded final score with 24.30/36. This preserves approximately the original 67.5% cutoff after removing LC-6's 7 points. Recalibrate after scoring a representative set of LinkedIn profiles.

The evaluator returns the tier, component points and supporting sources for all five criteria, including separate Google and Meta platform scores. For LC-5, include the metric keyword and, where available, the candidate’s action and measured change.

## 9. Non-Scoring Warnings and Review

Return Yes or No for each criterion’s Unverified flag. Use Yes when qualifying evidence is missing. For LC-4, mark Yes when the profile supplies no specific funnel details. For LC-5, mark Yes when no qualifying metric keyword is stated. For LC-1, set Unverified = Yes when neither platform has hands-on evidence, including profiles receiving platform-mention credit. Report an unverified second platform in Missing or Unclear Information when only one platform has hands-on evidence. For LC-3, mark Yes when no hands-on paid app or website campaign work is established, including profiles receiving mention-only credit.

Return Date-Quality Warning = Yes when dates cannot be used reliably under Section 7. Warnings do not change points.

Route to Review when neither Google nor Meta advertising platform is identified, when neither platform has hands-on evidence, when only one platform has hands-on evidence, or when the profile explicitly describes primarily product marketing, affiliate, organic or other-platform advertising work without demonstrated Google or Meta paid acquisition work.

Do not route to Review based on a minimum number of months or years of experience. A Review flag is not a rejection. Only profiles meeting the cutoff without a Review flag qualify for the automatic shortlist; other relevant profiles can be assessed manually.

Report whether the cutoff is met and whether Review is triggered as separate fields.

## 10. Ranking

Rank by unrounded Final Score, highest first. Equal totals share a rank. Display criterion scores and the Final Score to two decimal places. Keep the full precision for ranking and cutoff decisions so a score such as 24.30 is displayed consistently.

Always include Current Company and Current Title. Populate both from the same current Experience entry when the input identifies one. Treat an open-ended role as current when its source format uses a missing end date to indicate ongoing employment, the start date is valid, and the role is not explicitly marked as ended. Leave an unavailable or unverified value blank. These fields provide profile context and carry no points. Component points stay in the evaluator response.

Strongest Evidence — Up to three short facts separated by |. Identify the source role or profile section and the specific action or result.

Missing or Unclear Information — State the material gaps neutrally, such as the second platform, campaign ownership, creation or optimisation, campaign destination when unspecified, funnel results, paid campaign metrics or usable dates.

Score Rationale — In 50 words or fewer, explain the strongest evidence and the main uncertainty. Avoid listing every score.
